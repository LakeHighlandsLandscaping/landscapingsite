Mulch, Gravel & Rock Installation

Whether you're refreshing an existing landscape bed or creating a completely new one, we can handle the material preparation, installation, and cleanup from start to finish.

Material Selection

We can help you choose a material based on the look you're going for, the area being covered, maintenance needs, and your budget.

Bed Preparation

We prepare the area before installation by clearing debris, defining the bed, and getting the surface ready for the new material.

Material Installation

We spread and install mulch, gravel, or rock evenly throughout the designated area for a clean and finished appearance.

Cleanup

After installation, we clean up surrounding sidewalks, driveways, patios, and other areas so your property is left looking finished.

Material Options

Choose from a variety of mulch, gravel, and decorative rock options to create the look you want for your landscape.

**Material colors, sizes, and availability may vary depending on supplier and current inventory.**

Mulch

Mulch provides a clean, finished appearance while helping retain moisture and reduce weed growth in landscape beds.

Black Mulch

A dark, clean-looking mulch that creates strong contrast with green plants and flowers.

Hardwood Mulch

A natural-looking option that works well with a wide variety of plants and landscape styles.

Red Mulch

A bold, warm-colored mulch that adds contrast to landscape beds and creates a distinctive, vibrant look.

Gravel

Gravel can provide durable ground cover for landscape beds, pathways, drainage areas, and other outdoor spaces.

Pea Gravel

Small, rounded stones that create a clean and versatile surface for landscape beds, pathways, and other areas.

Black Tejas Gravel

Dark, natural stone that provides a clean, modern look for landscape beds, borders, and ground cover.

Utility Limestone Gravel

A light-colored crushed limestone commonly used for pathways, base material, drainage, and other practical landscaping applications.

Decorative Rock

Decorative rock adds long-lasting ground cover and can give landscape beds a natural or modern appearance.

River Rock

Smooth, rounded stones available in a variety of natural colors and sizes for landscape beds and drainage areas.

Black Lava Rock

Lightweight volcanic rock with a dark appearance that creates a modern look in landscape beds.

White Marble

Bright decorative stone that creates strong contrast and a clean appearance around plants and landscape beds.
