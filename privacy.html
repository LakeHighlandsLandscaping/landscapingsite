Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Lake Highlands Landscaping values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information when you use our website or request a free quote.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your property address
  • A description of the work you are requesting
  • Any additional information you choose to include

Our website hosting provider and form service may also collect limited technical information such as your browser type, IP address, and the date and time of your submission.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to quote requests
  • Contact you regarding your project
  • Schedule estimates or services
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services and website

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our quote request form is securely processed using Formspree, a third-party form processing service.

Information you submit through our website is transmitted to Formspree so that your request can be delivered to us. Formspree may store or process your information according to its own Privacy Policy.

You can review Formspree's Privacy Policy here:

https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide through our website. However, no method of transmitting information over the Internet or storing electronic information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

This website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or tracking.

Some third-party services, including GitHub Pages and Formspree, may use essential cookies or similar technologies necessary for website functionality.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for homeowners and property owners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
