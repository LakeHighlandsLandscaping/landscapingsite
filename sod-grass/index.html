Sod Varieties

Different grass varieties perform differently depending on sunlight, water, soil, and the conditions of your yard. We can help you choose an option that fits your property.

**Grass availability and varieties may vary depending on supplier and current inventory.**

St. Augustine

A popular warm-season grass in North Texas known for its broad blades and dense appearance.

St. Augustine generally performs best with adequate moisture and is commonly used in residential lawns.

Varieties of St. Augustine include Raleigh, which is the most common, and Palmetto, which is a shade-tolerant variety.

Bermuda

A warm-season grass known for its durability, fine texture, and ability to handle sunny areas.

Bermuda is commonly used for lawns that receive substantial direct sunlight and can tolerate regular foot traffic.

Zoysia

A dense warm-season grass with a fine-to-medium texture that can provide a thick, attractive lawn.

Zoysia can work well in a variety of residential conditions and generally requires less frequent mowing than some other grasses.
